Downloaded 16 November 2026 through the public Poly Haven asset API. Meshes, team marks, carbon weave, gravel and foliage textures are original procedural work in this repository. The car is inspired by 2025 ground-effect proportions; it is not a licensed replica of any team's car. No team CAD, extracted racing-game models are included.

## JGP–26 car GLBs (realism v2)

The editable car is in `scripts/blender/jgp26.blend`. Blender generates the player and grid meshes from project geometry. The bank-mark contours on the endplates and steering wheel were traced from the supplied front-corner frame. The car also includes lettering and sponsor marks observed in the supplied [SF-26 explorer video](https://x.com/reach_vb/status/2096271854193271229). Sponsor names and marks belong to their respective owners. Review images containing video frames are stored separately in `artifacts/vb-car`.
