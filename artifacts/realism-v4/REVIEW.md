## Blender and delivery fixes

Blender 5.2.2 LTS, factory startup, normal local Metal access, CPU Cycles AO at 32 samples. No replacement renderer or procedural Three.js car/circuit meshes. A slow deletion of tens of thousands of Blender instance objects was diagnosed at ~99% CPU; replaced operator deletion with `bpy.data.batch_remove`. Native glTF instancing initially exported nothing because instances were scene roots. Reading Blender's installed exporter showed it requires common-parent children; adding an identity parent produced native `EXT_mesh_gpu_instancing`. Runtime partitions those Blender instances into spatial batches for culling and nearby shadows.

MCP retains the strict host CSP. Gzip transport, 8-bit normalized normals and 16-bit circuit position attributes with uniform node transforms require no worker/WASM decoder there. Car positions retain float precision. Web circuits use gzip-wrapped Draco. A build-time check enforces every Worker static asset <=25 MiB. The source GLBs and editable .blend files are retained.

An early performance measurement overlapped Blender and MCP browser tests and fell below 60 FPS; an invitation assertion also timed out under that contention. Those were not accepted as final measurements. The first isolated run averaged about 71 FPS but dipped to 48 FPS in smoothed samples. Added Blender-authored far crown meshes at 220 m (with hysteresis), keeping the detailed near crowns. The isolated Monza rerun then measured 86.24 / 83.70 / 87.69 FPS means for Vector / Chaos / Broadcast, with lowest smoothed samples 71.65 / 66.72 / 73.82. Final benchmarks run independently after that asset build.

## Honest remaining gaps

- **Body:** smoother, with fewer overlapping paint surfaces, but white shoulder/airbox joins remain more visibly separate than the reference's integrated sculpt. Nose cross-section, intake lip and floor-edge aero remain an interpretation, not measured Ferrari surfaces.
- **Wings/wheels:** stacks, dishes, markings and rounded shoulders read better. Endplate twist and tiny fasteners still lack the reference's detail. Tyre flattening/bulging is a bounded visual response to telemetry, not a carcass simulation or a change to tyre forces.
- **Onboard:** live dash, mirrors, halo, stitched gloves and forearms are present. Hands are static sculpted geometry attached to the steering rig, not articulated anatomy; gloves and wrist transitions remain simplified. The low eye position exposes tyre tops but little of the front wing; T-cam reveals more.
- **Studio:** broad reflected softboxes, satin/clearcoat/flake variation, close-up carbon weave, baked AO and blurred floor reflection improve separation. This still lacks the reference's richer contact gradients and polished hand-authored surfacing; it is not path-traced photorealism.
- **Monza:** solid irregular, textured crowns replace crossing cards, with shared 3D branches and denser woodland. Crowns remain clustered forms rather than individual leaves; terrain planting and forest species distribution are approximate. Seated crowds have 3D heads/torsos but no faces or animation.
- **Vegas:** stepped/wrapped hotel masses, smaller windows, cornices/columns, roof plant and stairs reduce repetition. Buildings and pit structures are still generic interpretations. Wet-look road response is approximate; the city and Sphere do not have full screen-space/ray-traced reflections. Catch-fence mesh still uses a fine alpha texture between real wires/posts.
- **Accuracy:** authoritative track centreline, gameplay, radio, Jev/Luna, room/invite flows and rules are retained. The prior coarse DEM is still visual terrain, not surveyed road slopes or new gravity physics. Tests use mock brains, not live-provider performance.

## Final contact-sheet inspection

Reopened the final eleven car views and both four-view circuit sheets after the last camera/LOD/material fixes. Also reopened the front, steering, wheel and rear video comparisons and the circuit photograph sheets. The front nose still narrows sooner than the reference, the front-wing endplate/outer flap silhouette is less sculpted, and the white shoulder fairing has a visible seam. The rear shows a more separate airbox/fin assembly and simpler wing endplate twist. The wheel dish is too dark to reveal the reference’s rounded inner rim as clearly; generic lettering is intentionally different but also sparser. Steering controls follow the layout, though the button collars and rotaries read oversized and the carbon cockpit walls lack the reference’s subtle directional sheen. The straight-on live cockpit has a legible dash and working mirrors, but the rigid white glove/forearm silhouette still reads stylized.

Monza’s pit view lacks the real building’s latticework, busy balconies and dense human activity; repeated rounded crowns and sparse undergrowth remain conspicuous from aerial views. Vegas has more varied hotel silhouettes but large empty urban areas, simplified roof structures and bright bloom around floodlights. The Sphere artwork is original and much simpler than the real photograph. These are remaining fidelity gaps, not issues established as solved by passing gameplay tests.
